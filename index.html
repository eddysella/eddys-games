Eddy's Games — Support

A calm, fast, completely offline collection of puzzle and board games for iPhone.

Eddy's Games bundles a dozen familiar games behind a simple home screen — word puzzles, number puzzles, logic grids, and classic board games. No accounts, no ads, no internet required.

Frequently asked questions

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Eddy's Games is fully offline. It needs no internet connection and makes no network requests — play on a plane, on the subway, or anywhere with no signal.

How do the daily puzzles work?

Several games offer a Daily puzzle. Everyone gets the same daily puzzle for a given day, generated right on your device — there's no server involved. A new daily puzzle becomes available each day. You can also play Unlimited mode for endless puzzles.

Where are my scores and leaderboards stored?

All scores, records, streaks, and leaderboards are stored locally on your device only. They are never uploaded anywhere — your stats are private to you, and they live on this device (not synced between devices).

How do I reset my progress or scores?

Open the app's Settings to clear records and progress. To remove everything, delete the app from your iPhone — iOS erases all of its locally stored data.

How do I turn the daily reminder on or off?

The daily reminder is optional and off by default. Enable or disable it in the app's Settings. The first time you enable it, iOS asks for notification permission. You can also manage it in the iOS Settings app under Notifications.

Is the app accessible?

Yes. Eddy's Games works with iOS accessibility features including VoiceOver and Dynamic Type (larger text), and aims never to rely on color alone to convey information.

Does the app collect any personal data?

No. No personal data, no accounts, no analytics, no third-party trackers. See the privacy policy for full details.

What devices are supported?

Eddy's Games is an iPhone app and requires iOS 17 or later. It runs in portrait orientation.
